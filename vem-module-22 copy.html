Module 22: Systems & Tools — VEM Deep Dive

Deep dive into Salesforce, Airtable, and your data and reporting responsibilities.

You covered the general overview of PTC's tools in Module 4. This module goes deep on the two systems that are central to your daily work as VEM — Salesforce and Airtable — and covers your data and reporting responsibilities.

Salesforce — Your Primary System

Volunteer TrackingLog all mentor applicant information, track screening status (application, interview, references, background check), and mark match readiness. Every step of the pipeline lives here.
Mentor/Mentee MatchesTrack active matches, match health, and meeting consistency. The Mentor Meetings Dashboard gives you a real-time view of engagement.
Interview DocumentationInterview questions are completed and documented directly in Salesforce — not in external documents.
Reference ChecksReference requests are sent and collected through Salesforce. Check here to confirm references are complete before moving forward.
Grant ReportingPull your monthly grant metrics from Salesforce dashboards. Accurate data entry is critical to maintaining funder reporting.
🔔 Hands-on Salesforce training is part of your onboarding. Schedule this with the Programs Team during your first two weeks.

Airtable — Weekly Updates & Events

Weekly UpdatesMentors submit their weekly updates via Airtable form every week. You review these submissions and data flows into your Salesforce dashboards. Missing submissions = red flag to follow up immediately.
EventsEvent management and survey collection are also handled through Airtable. You'll add events and manage survey responses here.

Key Metrics You Track Monthly

Active Matches

Number of active mentor/mentee matches currently running.

Volunteer Hours

Total volunteer hours logged per month across all matches.

Retention Rate

How many matches continue from month to month.

New Mentors Onboarded

Number of new mentors who completed screening and matched this month.

Keep running notes throughout the month. Grant reporting is monthly or quarterly — it should never be a last-minute scramble. Notes = easy reporting.
