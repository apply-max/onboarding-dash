Expensify — Expense & Receipt Management SOP

Everything you need to know to submit expenses correctly, on time, and audit-ready.

Path to College uses Expensify to manage all company card expenses. Transactions import automatically from your Capital One Spark card, and your job is to upload receipts, categorize correctly, and submit on time. This SOP walks you through every step and covers the most common mistakes to avoid.

⚡ Core Principles
⚡

Real-Time Submission

Submit as you spend — never batch at month-end. Receipts fade, memories fade faster.

🧾

Receipt Required

Every expense needs a valid receipt. No exceptions — no receipt means not approved.

🎯

Accuracy First

Expensify's AI mislabels categories often. Always verify before submitting.

📝

Clear Descriptions

Explain the business purpose clearly. Who, what, why — every single time.

🏦

Expensify = Source of Truth

No more Google Drive uploads. Everything lives in Expensify, period.

📅 Deadlines That Matter

Missing these affects board reporting and financial close.

Receipt Upload → Within 24 hours

Upload your receipt the same day you make the purchase. Don't let it sit.

Expense Coding → Weekly

Set a weekly 15–30 minute "expense block" — Friday afternoons work well. Review, code, and organize into a report.

Month-End Close → First week of next month

Non-negotiable. All expenses for the prior month must be submitted and in Maria's queue by the first few days of the following month. This feeds directly into board financial reporting.

✅ Do & Don't

Do

  • Submit expenses in real time
  • Take a receipt photo immediately after purchase
  • Write descriptive, specific expense descriptions
  • Double-check the AI-assigned category
  • Organize expenses into weekly named reports
  • Ask Maria if you're unsure about anything
  • Set a weekly recurring "expense block" on your calendar

Don't

  • Wait until end of month to submit
  • Submit without attaching a receipt
  • Create manual expenses for card charges
  • Leave descriptions vague (e.g., just "lunch")
  • Change the Merchant field
  • Check the Reimbursable box for company cards
  • Guess on category — when in doubt, ask
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What if my expense hasn't shown up yet?▼
Wait 24–36 hours — card transactions have a delay before they auto-import. If it's still missing after that window, notify Maria. The most common cause is that the card needs relinking in Expensify.
What if I lost my receipt?▼
Upload an alternative — a bank or card statement showing the charge, or a forwarded confirmation email. Add an explanation note in the description field. Approval is not guaranteed — prevention is the best policy.
Why can't I edit an approved expense?▼
Once approved, the expense may already be exported to QuickBooks. Changes require admin or bookkeeper intervention to keep both systems in sync. This is why getting it right before submitting matters so much.
Why is my category showing as "Legal/Professional"?▼
Expensify's AI frequently mislabels expenses — "Legal/Professional" is one of the most common wrong auto-assignments. Always manually correct the category before submitting. Never trust the AI default.
Should I ever manually create an expense?▼
No — not for company card charges. Manually creating an expense when a card charge already exists triggers a reimbursement instead of matching the existing charge. Only create manual expenses if Maria has specifically instructed you to.
What should I put in the Description field?▼
Think: Who + What + Why.
• "Lunch with donor to discuss scholarship funding" ✓
• "Office supplies for EdFest volunteer packets" ✓
• "Parent/student meeting – college tour meal" ✓
• "Lunch" ✗ — too vague, will be flagged
What happens if I don't submit on time?▼
Late submissions delay financial reporting, impact board financials, and hold up the accounting close process for the entire organization. The month-end deadline is non-negotiable — missing it creates extra work for Maria and the bookkeeper.
