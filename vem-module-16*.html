Module 16: Mentor Program Overview

A community-based one-on-one mentorship model pairing each fellow with a vetted adult mentor.

Program at a Glance

1:1
Match ratio
2–3
Years per match
4×
Meetings/month min.
1 hr
Per meeting minimum
In-person meetings are strongly encouraged. Virtual meetings can be used when needed, but the relationship thrives on face-to-face connection.

What Mentors Help Students Navigate

College Exploration

Identifying schools, understanding requirements, and building a thoughtful college list.

Career Exposure

Sharing professional experience and opening doors to real-world opportunities.

Goal Setting

Working with students to set and pursue meaningful academic and personal goals.

Accountability

Showing up consistently and holding students to their commitments.

Confidence Building

Being a steady, encouraging presence throughout all of high school.

Weekly Mentor Responsibilities

  • Meet consistently — minimum 4 times per month
  • Maintain professional boundaries at all times
  • Follow transportation and safety policies — no private homes, no driving until cleared
  • Communicate concerns to PTC staff promptly
  • Submit a weekly update via Airtable form — every week, no exceptions

Why Weekly Updates Matter

Program Supervision

Primary way the team supervises relationships and catches issues early.

Match Health

Visibility into meeting consistency, student progress, and challenges needing follow-up.

Grant Reporting

Contribute to monthly and quarterly funder reporting — accurate data is critical.

Early Intervention

Low or missing submissions are a red flag. Follow up before a gap becomes disengagement.
