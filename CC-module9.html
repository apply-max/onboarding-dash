Module 9: 15-Passenger Van Safety Training

Complete your required van safety training and submit your assessment diploma.

This module covers your required 15-Passenger Van Safety Training. This training is required for all approved drivers at Path to College. Please read the instructions carefully and follow all steps to receive In Compliance status.

Training Details

Training Title
15-Passenger Van Safety Training [Virtual]
Training Provider
UL Solutions
Training Length
Approx. 20 Minutes
Training Renewal
One-time
Training Status
⚠️ REQUIRED for Approved Drivers
📋 Account Access: You will be assigned a username and login from OSHA Outreach Courses. Watch for an email with your credentials before beginning. If you have not received your login, contact Operations.

How to Complete This Training

Please complete all of the following steps to receive In Compliance status.

  1. 1
    Log in to your OSHA Outreach Courses account using the username and credentials that were assigned to you.
  2. 2
    Select the assigned 15-Passenger Van Safety training from your dashboard.
  3. 3
    Complete the training, then complete the training assessment. You must pass the assessment for compliance credit.
  4. 4
    Once you have completed the assessment, you will be asked if you'd like to print your diploma.
    🎓 Required: You must print or save your assessment diploma as a PDF. This diploma is required to receive compliance credit — do not skip this step.
  5. 5
    Check off completion in BambooHR. In the pop-up, enter your completion date and upload your assessment diploma.
⚠️

Please Note: Failure to complete Steps 4 and 5 will result in receiving an OUT OF COMPLIANCE employee training status. Make sure to save your diploma and upload it to BambooHR before marking complete.

Ready to Sign Your Acknowledgment?

Once you have fully completed the training, passed the assessment, and submitted your diploma, return to BambooHR to sign your Module 9 acknowledgment.

1
Complete all 5 training steps above and save your assessment diploma.
2
Log in to BambooHR and navigate to your onboarding section.
3
Enter your completion date and upload your diploma.
4
Sign the Module 9 acknowledgment to confirm completion.
