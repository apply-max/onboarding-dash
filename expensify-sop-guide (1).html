Deadlines That Matter

Missing these affects board reporting and financial close.

Receipt Upload → Within 24 hours

Upload your receipt the same day you make the purchase. Don't let it sit.

Expense Coding → Weekly

Set a weekly 15–30 minute "expense block" — Friday afternoons work well. Review, code, and organize into a report.

Month-End Close → First week of next month

Non-negotiable. All expenses for the prior month must be submitted and in Maria's queue by the first few days of the following month. This feeds directly into board financial reporting.

Do & Don't

Common mistakes and the right habits to build instead.

✓ Do

  • Submit expenses in real time
  • Take a receipt photo immediately after purchase
  • Write descriptive, specific expense descriptions
  • Double-check the AI-assigned category
  • Organize expenses into weekly named reports
  • Ask Maria if you're unsure about anything
  • Set a weekly recurring "expense block" on your calendar

✕ Don't

  • Wait until end of month to submit
  • Submit without attaching a receipt
  • Create manual expenses for card charges
  • Leave descriptions vague (e.g., just "lunch")
  • Change the Merchant field
  • Check the Reimbursable box for company cards
  • Guess on category — when in doubt, ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question to expand the answer.

What if my expense hasn't shown up yet? ▼
Wait 24–36 hours — card transactions have a delay before they auto-import. If it's still missing after that window, notify Maria. The most common cause is that the card needs relinking in Expensify.
What if I lost my receipt? ▼
Upload an alternative proof — a bank or card statement showing the charge, or a forwarded confirmation email. Add an explanation note in the description field explaining why the original receipt isn't available. Approval is not guaranteed in this case — prevention is the best policy.
Why can't I edit an approved expense? ▼
Once approved, the expense may already be exported to QuickBooks. Changes at that point require admin or bookkeeper intervention to keep the two systems in sync. This is why getting it right before submitting matters so much.
Why is my category showing as "Legal/Professional"? ▼
Expensify's AI frequently mislabels expenses — "Legal/Professional" is one of the most common wrong auto-assignments. This is normal. Always manually correct the category before submitting. Never trust the AI default.
Should I ever manually create an expense? ▼
No — not for company card charges. Manually creating an expense when a card charge already exists (or will arrive) triggers a reimbursement instead of matching the existing charge. Only create manual expenses if Maria has specifically instructed you to for a particular situation.
What should I put in the Description field? ▼
Think: Who + What + Why. Examples:
• "Lunch with donor to discuss scholarship funding" ✓
• "Office supplies for EdFest volunteer packets" ✓
• "Parent/student meeting – college tour meal" ✓
• "Lunch" ✗ — too vague, will be flagged
Can I upload multiple receipts to one expense? ▼
Yes, you can add multiple attachments to a single expense. However, there is one primary receipt — this should be the clearest, most complete version. Additional attachments are supplemental. To delete an incorrect receipt, use the three-dot menu. You may need to exit and re-enter the expense to see the delete option appear.
What happens if I don't submit on time? ▼
Late submissions delay financial reporting, impact board financials, and can hold up the accounting close process for the entire organization. The month-end deadline in the first week of the following month is non-negotiable — missing it creates extra work for Maria and the bookkeeper.

Team Best Practices

Small habits that make a big difference at month-end.

📅

Weekly Expense Block

Block 15–30 minutes on your calendar each Friday to review, code, and submit that week's expenses.

📱

Phone Receipt Album

Create a dedicated "Receipts 2026" photo album. Screenshot or photo every receipt directly into it.

📧

Forward Email Receipts

Email receipts to receipts@expensify.com immediately. This creates a backup and speeds up matching.

🙋

Ask Don't Guess

Unsure about a category, class, or project field? Ask Maria before submitting — it's faster than fixing it after approval.
