Purpose

This policy ensures that Path to College employees understand their roles in an emergency and that the organization can function successfully after an emergency has passed, returning quickly to normal operations. It applies to all employees and describes objectives and procedures for both preparation and actual emergency and disaster situations.

Key Policies to Know

  • ●Data Backup: All electronic files are backed up on Google Drive. Never store organization data on systems that are not backed up. Backups occur via cloud storage and physical media daily.
  • ●Critical Passwords: A list of critical passwords is housed on the Trello platform.
  • ●Physical Records: Important physical documents are stored in hazard-resistant containers. When possible, a scanned digital copy is maintained and backed up.
  • ●Your Role: All employees are responsible for understanding their role in an emergency and responding in accordance with organization policies.
