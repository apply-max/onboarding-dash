Module 3: Policies & Procedures

Understand our workplace guidelines, expectations, and how we operate.

Our policies and procedures are designed to create a safe, respectful, and productive work environment. This module outlines key workplace policies and expectations to help you understand how we operate and what's expected of you. By the end of this module, you'll feel confident navigating our organizational policies and equipped to work efficiently within our framework.

Work Hours & Timekeeping

Regular office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Specific days and times may vary based on your role and Path to College's calendar.

Timekeeping System: BambooHR — All exempt and nonexempt employees must log hours worked and PTO requests using BambooHR. You'll learn more in Module 4.

PTO (Paid Time Off) and Holidays

Full-time, salaried employees receive paid time off according to the following accrual schedule:

TenurePTO Days
After 90 days of employment15 days
After 1 year of employment20 days
After 2+ years of employment25 days

PTO days do not roll over and there are no payouts for unused PTO.

Dress Code

Smart Casual Attire: Employees should dress in business casual attire unless the day's activities require otherwise. Employees should always dress appropriately for the possibility of meeting mentors, donors, or business partners.

💡

Need inspiration? Check out the PTC Smart Casual Work Fashion Pinterest board.

View Pinterest Board →

Athletic Wear: Fitness or athletic clothing is only permitted for specific tasks, such as outdoor service days or fitness events.

General Guidelines:

  • Clothing must be neat, well-maintained, and free of holes, tears, or visible wear.
  • Clothing with offensive or inappropriate graphics, language, or designs is prohibited.
  • Attire should not be excessively revealing or inappropriate for a professional setting.
  • Styles associated with religious or cultural practices are fully respected and exempt from standard guidelines.
