⚠️ Recognizing a Cyber Incident

A cyber incident may include:

  • A suspicious email requesting personal or company information.
  • Unauthorized access or login attempts on company accounts.
  • Loss or theft of company devices containing sensitive information.
  • Detection of malware, ransomware, or other security threats.
  • Accidental sharing or exposure of confidential data.

If you suspect a cyber incident, follow these steps:

Stop & Disconnect

If possible, disconnect the device from Wi-Fi to prevent further spread.

Do Not Engage

Do NOT reply to suspicious emails, click unknown links, or download attachments from unverified sources.

Report It

Immediately notify your Manager and follow the reporting guidelines within the Cyber Incident Response Plan.
