Module 2: Welcome & Introduction

Learn who we are, what we stand for, and how your role fits our mission.

Our work is rooted in the belief that every young person deserves access to education and opportunities that ignite their potential. This module provides an overview of Path to College, our mission, values, and the people who make our work possible. By the end of this module, you'll have a strong understanding of who we are, what we do, and how your role fits into our mission.

Our Mission, Vision, and Values

Mission

Expand minds and ignite potential.

Vision

We envision a world where genius, leadership, and excellence are cultivated in the most overlooked students in our community.

Key Beliefs:

  • We believe every child has untapped brilliance and the potential to achieve greatness when given the right support, resources, and encouragement.
  • We are committed to breaking the cycle of poverty by providing students with unwavering mentorship, expert guidance, and financial support to pursue post-secondary education.
  • We understand the challenges students face: a lack of support, limited access to a culture of success, and fear of navigating the complicated path to college admissions.
  • We are dedicated to being a guiding light for students by offering a "north star" through mentorship, motivation, college preparation, life skills, and extracurricular opportunities.
  • We strive to ignite the potential in students and prepare the next generation of scholars, leaders, and entrepreneurs by creating a positive culture of success.
  • We believe in the transformative power of education to improve individual lives, strengthen communities, and create a more equitable society.

Our Story

In 2017, Path to College was founded on a donation and a dream. While a teacher, founder Christine Sylvain saw firsthand the disparities between high school students in well-resourced schools from college-going homes and their peers in Title 1 schools. Both groups had the same intelligence and aspirations, but the Title 1 group carried a double burden with no support. Path to College was created to support this group and prevent the loss of a precious and valuable resource — a generation of students who belong at college and deserve the benefits of a high-quality education.
