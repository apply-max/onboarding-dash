Module 7: Florida Professionally Mandated Reporter

Complete your required compliance training and submit your certificate.

This module covers your required Florida Professionally Mandated Reporter training. As a staff member, you are legally required to complete this training. Please read the instructions carefully and follow all steps to receive In Compliance status.

Training Details

Training Title
Florida Professionally Mandated Reporter [Virtual]
Training Provider
Florida Department of Children and Families
Training Length
Approx. 2 Hours
Training Renewal
One-time
Training Status
⚠️ REQUIRED for Programs Department Staff
🎓

Ready to begin? Click the button below to access the Florida Professionally Mandated Reporter training. You can also find the link directly in BambooHR.

Start Training →

How to Complete This Training

Please complete all of the following steps to receive In Compliance status.

  1. 1
    Click the training link in BambooHR or use the Start Training button above.
  2. 2
    Click the play button and follow the on-screen prompts.
  3. 3
    Complete the training, then complete the training assessment. You must pass the assessment for compliance credit. Once the training is complete, click the Lead the Way icon and then select the Demonstrate method. This will bring you to the course completion exam.
  4. 4
    Once you have completed the assessment and are on the certificate screen, be sure to click Print, then save the certificate as a PDF. To receive credit for completing this training, you must save and submit the certificate.
  5. 5
    Check off completion of the training in BambooHR. You will see a pop-up — enter the completion date and upload your assessment certificate PDF.
⚠️

Please Note: Failure to complete Steps 4 and 5 will result in receiving an OUT OF COMPLIANCE employee training status. Make sure to save your certificate and upload it to BambooHR before marking complete.

Ready to Sign Your Acknowledgment?

Once you have fully completed the training and submitted your certificate, return to BambooHR to sign your Module 7 acknowledgment.

1
Complete all 5 training steps above and save your certificate PDF.
2
Log in to BambooHR and navigate to your onboarding section.
3
Enter your completion date and upload your certificate.
4
Sign the Module 7 acknowledgment to confirm completion.
