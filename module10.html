Module 10: The Phone Call That Breached the Company

Learn how attackers use phone calls to manipulate employees — and how to stop them.

Not every cyberattack starts with malware. Some begin with a single phone call. Review the training slides below, then complete the acknowledgment form at the bottom of the page.

Key Takeaways

  • Vishing attacks use urgency and false familiarity — slow down before acting.
  • No legitimate vendor will ever ask for your login or MFA code over the phone.
  • It is always acceptable to say "I need to verify this" and hang up.
  • Always call back using the official number — never one the caller gives you.
  • Report suspicious calls immediately to your manager.

Next Steps

  • Proceed to Training 2: Phishing Awareness to learn how email-based attacks work.
  • If you ever receive a suspicious call, report it to your manager immediately.
