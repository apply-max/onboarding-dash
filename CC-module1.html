Module 1: Introduction to the Onboarding Program

Start here — learn what to expect and how to navigate your onboarding journey.

Hi, and welcome to Path to College! We're excited to have you join us as we work together to expand minds and ignite potential. This onboarding experience is designed to help you transition smoothly into your role, understand our mission, and feel equipped to succeed. Let's explore what you can expect!

Purpose of the Onboarding Program

  • Provide you with a clear understanding of our mission, values, and goals.
  • Familiarize you with key policies, procedures, technology, and tools.
  • Equip you with the knowledge and skills needed to thrive in your role.
  • Help you integrate into our team and organizational culture.

How to Navigate the Onboarding Program

  • Each module is self-guided and designed to be completed at your own pace.
  • Activities and quizzes are included to reinforce learning.
  • Keep a notebook or document handy to jot down questions for your manager or team.

Expected Outcomes

✓ Understand how your role contributes to our mission.
✓ Feel confident navigating our systems and tools.
✓ Be familiar with our team and organizational culture.
✓ Be ready to start making an impact in your role.
