Module 8: ESET Cybersecurity Awareness Training

Complete your required annual cybersecurity training and submit your completion screenshot.

This module covers your required ESET Cybersecurity Awareness Training. As a Path to College employee, you are required to complete this training annually. Please read the instructions carefully and follow all steps to receive In Compliance status.

How to Complete This Training

Please complete all of the following steps to receive In Compliance status.

  1. 1
    Click the training link in BambooHR or use the Start Training button above.
  2. 2
    Fill in the required registration information:
    • For "How will you be using this training?" select Taking it myself / as an employee.
    • For "I agree to receive marketing emails from ESET" select No.
  3. 3
    Click the green Register Now button.
  4. 4
    Complete all training modules including Working Remotely. Module boxes will start blue and turn gray once completed.
  5. 5
    Once all modules are complete, you will receive a Congratulations! completion message on screen.
    📸 Required: Take a screenshot of the screen showing the Congratulations / completion message. You must submit this screenshot to receive compliance credit.
  6. 6
    Check off completion in BambooHR. In the pop-up, enter your completion date and upload the screenshot of your completion message.
⚠️

Please Note: Failure to complete Steps 5 and 6 will result in receiving an OUT OF COMPLIANCE employee training status. Make sure to screenshot your completion message and upload it to BambooHR before marking complete.

Ready to Sign Your Acknowledgment?

Once you have fully completed all training modules and submitted your screenshot, return to BambooHR to sign your Module 8 acknowledgment.
