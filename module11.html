Module 11: Phishing Awareness

Learn how to recognize, investigate, and report phishing and spear phishing attacks.

Phishing is one of the most common ways attackers gain access to organizations — and it targets people, not just systems. This module walks you through how phishing attacks work, what red flags to look for, and exactly what to do if you suspect you've received one. Key Takeaways

  • Phishing targets many people broadly; spear phishing targets specific individuals with higher stakes.
  • Attackers research their targets before striking — familiarity does not mean legitimacy.
  • Red flags include urgency, credential requests, payment requests, and identity inconsistencies.
  • Always pause and verify independently before acting on any unexpected request.
  • If you suspect a scam: stop, document, report to Maria at maria@pathtocollege.org, and protect yourself.
